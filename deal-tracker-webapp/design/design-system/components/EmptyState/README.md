# EmptyState

What a list shows before it has anything in it: a calm blue "radar" mark, a short title, one or two sentences and one action, on `gradient-wash`.

- Title states the fact: "No trackers yet", "No offers found yet".
- Body says what happens next: "We check every 30 minutes by default."
- One action, usually primary ("Track your first site"); omit it when there's nothing to do but wait.
- The mark is fixed; there is no orange in an empty state.
